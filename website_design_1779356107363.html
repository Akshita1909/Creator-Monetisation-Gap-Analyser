Methodology & Metrics

Transparent Signals

Metrics

subscriber_count, views, video_count, avg_views, engagement_rate, upload_frequency, channel_age_days, niche, country.

Revenue Proxy

avg_views × 0.003 USD per view to estimate baseline earnings.

Efficiency

actual_revenue / expected_for_subscriber_count for gap scoring.

Cohorts

10k–50k, 50k–500k, 500k+ subscribers with tailored insights.
